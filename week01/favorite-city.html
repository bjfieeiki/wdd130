Favorite City

My favorite city is Nuku'alofa. It is located in the Tonga islands in the Kingdom of Tonga in the south pacific ocean. Nuku'alofa is the capital city and it is where the King's palace is located. There is also an international airport on the island and tourists from all over the world come to visit Tonga every year.

One of my favorite sites in Nuku'alofa is the LDS Nuku'alofa Tonga Temple. Tonga is a professed Christian nation and every Sunday, the holy sabbath day is observed and all businesses are closed. Many blessings have come because of this practice including protection from cyclones, tsunamis, volcanic explosions, etc. Faith plays an important role in everyday life in Tonga.
